Why extra payments help

MechanismInterest is charged only on the balance still owing. Every extra dollar goes straight onto the principal rather than future interest, so the balance shrinks faster and each later period is charged interest on a smaller amount. Over time this compounds: a shorter loan term and less total interest paid, even though the extra amount itself seems small per payment.
